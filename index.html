HarvestShield Research prototype. Not a licensed insurance product.

When the rain fails, the payout shouldn't wait for a claim form.

Pick a farm and a planting date. HarvestShield replays 30 years of real rainfall and crop water demand, estimates the yield hit in each season using FAO methods, and shows what a policy would have paid and what that cover should cost.

    Weather data: Open-Meteo.com (ERA5 reanalysis, CC BY 4.0). Place search by Open-Meteo geocoding. Crop and soil parameters: FAO Irrigation and Drainage Papers 56 and 33, listed with links on the results page.

    Reproducible: every figure can be rebuilt from the public data with the verification script and CSV export that ship with the source code.

    This is a simulation for learning and research. It is not insurance, and real cover in Nigeria must come from a NAICOM-licensed insurer.
